Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Founder of the Maratha Empire • Great Warrior • Visionary King

About The Great King

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was one of India's greatest rulers and the founder of the Maratha Empire. Born on 19 February 1630 at Shivneri Fort in Maharashtra, he displayed courage, intelligence, and leadership from an early age. Under the guidance of his mother, Jijabai, and his mentor, Dadoji Konddev, he developed strong values of justice, bravery, and respect for all religions.

Shivaji Maharaj built a powerful kingdom based on good governance, military strength, and the welfare of his people. He captured several forts through brilliant planning and introduced innovative guerrilla warfare tactics, which helped his smaller army defeat much larger enemies. His strategic thinking made him one of the finest military leaders in Indian history.

In 1674, Shivaji Maharaj was crowned as the Chhatrapati at Raigad Fort, marking the establishment of the Maratha Empire. He promoted efficient administration, encouraged trade, strengthened the navy, and ensured the safety and dignity of women and civilians during times of war. His leadership inspired unity and self-rule among his people.

Even centuries after his lifetime, Shivaji Maharaj remains a symbol of courage, patriotism, and justice. His life continues to inspire millions of people to lead with integrity, determination, and compassion while serving their nation and society.

Major Achievements

1630

Born at Shivneri Fort near Junnar, Maharashtra.

1645

Captured Torna Fort, beginning the expansion of the Maratha Kingdom.

1659

Defeated Afzal Khan through brilliant planning and courage.

1664

Successfully led the famous campaign at Surat, strengthening the Maratha treasury.

1674

Crowned as Chhatrapati at Raigad Fort and established the Maratha Empire.

1680

Passed away, leaving behind a legacy of bravery, justice, and visionary leadership.
